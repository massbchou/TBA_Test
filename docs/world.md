# The Lantern Road: World Bible

## Premise

The kingdom of **Vael** is being swallowed by **the Dusk**: a slow grey twilight that creeps in from the
east and hollows out whatever it settles on. Fields go silent. People become *Hollows*, still walking,
no longer anyone. Nobody knows what the Dusk is. Everyone knows it is coming.

The last way west is the **Lantern Road**, an old pilgrim road lit by waystation fires that the
**Lantern Keepers** have tended for three hundred years. Light still holds the Dusk back, a little. You
are a lone traveller walking the Road: one floor is one stretch between waystations. At the end of
the first stretch stands **Ashgate**, the first of the old gates, and something that was set to guard it
long ago.

## Tone

Melancholy but not hopeless. The world is dying slowly and people are mostly kind about it. Humour comes
from travellers making do. The Dusk is never explained, only described. Text is short and concrete:
one or two sentences per beat.

## Regions along the first stretch

| Floors | Region | Feel | Enemies (tag) |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| 1–3 | **Brackenford** riverlands | Ferries, reed beds, the first abandoned farms | Dusk Rats, Road Bandits, Lantern Moths, Mire Toads (`early`) |
| 4–6 | **Saltmere** marsh coast | Drowned chapels, fog, stilt villages | Hollows, Marsh Lurkers, Gloom Bats, Spore Caps (`mid`) |
| 7–9 | **Cinderhold** hills | Shrine towns, old battlefields, warm ash | Ash Wolves, Dusk-Touched Knights, Cinder Imps (`late`) |
| 10 | **Ashgate** | The first gate | The Ashgate Warden or the Mother of Moths |

Also mentioned: **Greyhallow** (a scholars' city to the north, source of Mages and books) and
**Thornwick** (a market town of gamblers and pickpockets).

## Factions and figures

- **Lantern Keepers.** Quiet caretakers of the waystation fires. They trade, heal, and never leave the
  Road.
- **Bandits of the Road.** People who decided that the end of the world is a business opportunity.
- **The Hollow.** Dusk-touched people. Not evil; empty. Some still repeat the last thing they cared about.

## Classes

Four classic roles, following Dragon Quest's core party:

| Class | Origin | Fighting style | Resource |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Warrior | A sellsword from Brackenford's ferry guard | Sword; heavy hits that exploit **Sunder** | Stamina |
| Martial Artist | A Thornwick fight-pit champion | Fists; **Mark** a target, then finish it | Stamina |
| Priest | A Keeper-trained healer from Cinderhold | Staff and light; heals and sustain | Mana (from WIS), some stamina |
| Mage | A Greyhallow scholar who left before the Dusk took the libraries | Fire, ice, lightning; siphons mana | Mana |

## Elements and weapons

- **Elements:** FIRE, ICE, LIGHTNING, LIGHT, DARK.
- **Physical types (reduced by Defense):** PHYSICAL (fists, claws), SWORD, STAFF.
- **Dusk creatures** are usually weak to LIGHT and resist DARK. Armoured foes resist SWORD and are
  weak to STAFF.
